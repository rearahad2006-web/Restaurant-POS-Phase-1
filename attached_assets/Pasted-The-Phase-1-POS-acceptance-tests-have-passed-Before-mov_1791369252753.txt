- Card sales

- Delivery
- Tables

### Orders

- Order number

## 3. Payment and Billing Requirements

IMPORTANT:

The customer's receipt and the internal payment record are two separate things.

When an order is paid:

1. Save the completed order.
2. Save the payment record.
3. Save the payment method.
4. Save cash received and change if applicable.
5. Generate receipt data.
6. Show receipt.
7. Allow printing/reprinting.

Do not lose payment information after the receipt is closed.

## 4. Manager Dashboard Totals

The dashboard totals must come from actual saved orders/payments.

Do not use fake hard-coded statistics.

Example:

If:
Cash = PKR 1,010
Card = PKR 850
Easypaisa = PKR 610
JazzCash = PKR 1,400

Then:

Total sales = PKR 3,870.
